EXAMINATION POLICY 2024
=======================

Document ID: EXAM-POL-2024
Version: 1.0
Effective From: 2024-07-01
Issued By: Examination Controller
Authority Level: 2

SECTION 1: EXAMINATION SCHEDULE
--------------------------------

1.1 Semester Pattern
The university follows a semester system with:
- Two major semesters (Odd and Even) per academic year
- Each semester is 18 weeks long
- Mid-semester exams in week 9
- End-semester exams after week 18

1.2 Examination Calendar
- Mid-semester exams: 20% weightage
- End-semester exams: 50% weightage
- Continuous assessment: 30% weightage


SECTION 2: EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY
-----------------------------------

2.1 Attendance Requirement
As per Academic Regulations, students must have minimum 75% attendance to appear for end-semester examinations.

2.2 Fee Clearance
All semester fees and dues must be cleared at least 15 days before the commencement of examinations.

2.3 Course Registration
Only courses for which students have completed registration will be valid for examination.


SECTION 3: EXAMINATION CONDUCT
-------------------------------

3.1 Entry Requirements
Students must:
- Bring valid university ID card
- Arrive 15 minutes before exam start
- Not carry any unauthorized materials

3.2 Prohibited Items
The following are strictly prohibited in examination halls:
- Mobile phones and electronic devices
- Books and notes (except in open-book exams)
- Programmable calculators (unless specified)

3.3 Malpractice
Any form of malpractice will result in:
- Cancellation of exam
- Zero marks for the course
- Possible disciplinary action including suspension


SECTION 4: ANSWER SHEETS AND EVALUATION
----------------------------------------

4.1 Evaluation Timeline
Answer sheets will be evaluated within 15 days of examination completion.

4.2 Result Declaration
Results will be declared within 30 days of the last examination.

4.3 Re-evaluation
Students may apply for re-evaluation within 7 days of result declaration. Re-evaluation fee is Rs. 500 per course.


SECTION 5: SUPPLEMENTARY EXAMINATIONS
--------------------------------------

5.1 Eligibility
Students who fail in up to 3 courses may appear for supplementary examinations.

5.2 Schedule
Supplementary exams are conducted within 45 days of regular result declaration.

5.3 Limitations
- Only one supplementary attempt per course per semester
- Grades obtained cannot exceed B grade
- Supplementary exams do not apply to project/internship courses


END OF DOCUMENT
