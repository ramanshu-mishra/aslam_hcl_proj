ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2024
=========================

Document ID: ACAD-REG-2024
Version: 1.0
Effective From: 2024-07-01
Issued By: Dean of Academics
Authority Level: 1

SECTION 1: ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------------------

1.1 Minimum Attendance
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.

1.2 Medical Exemptions
Students with valid medical certificates may be granted exemptions from up to 10% of total classes. Such cases must be submitted within 7 days of absence.

1.3 Shortage Consequences
Students with attendance below 75% will not be permitted to appear for exams unless they obtain special permission from the Dean of Academics.


SECTION 2: GRADING SYSTEM
--------------------------

2.1 Grade Scale
The university follows a 10-point CGPA scale with the following grade distribution:

A+ (Outstanding): 9.0-10.0
A (Excellent): 8.0-8.9
B+ (Very Good): 7.0-7.9
B (Good): 6.0-6.9
C+ (Average): 5.0-5.9
C (Pass): 4.0-4.9
F (Fail): Below 4.0

2.2 CGPA Calculation
The Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) is calculated as the weighted average of grade points across all completed courses, with course credits as weights.

2.3 Minimum CGPA Requirements
- For continuation: Minimum CGPA of 5.0 required
- For graduation: Minimum CGPA of 6.0 required
- For honors degree: Minimum CGPA of 7.5 required


SECTION 3: EXAMINATION RULES
-----------------------------

3.1 Eligibility
To be eligible for examinations, students must:
- Have minimum 75% attendance
- Have paid all fees
- Have no pending disciplinary actions

3.2 Re-examination
Students who fail a course may re-appear for the examination in the next available semester. A maximum of 3 attempts are allowed per course.

3.3 Grade Improvement
Students may apply for grade improvement for courses where they obtained grades below B. Only one improvement attempt is allowed per course.


SECTION 4: CREDIT REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------------

4.1 Graduation Credits
To graduate with a Bachelor's degree, students must complete:
- Minimum 120 credits for 3-year programs
- Minimum 160 credits for 4-year programs

4.2 Course Load
- Minimum course load per semester: 15 credits
- Maximum course load per semester: 24 credits
- Students with CGPA above 8.0 may take up to 27 credits with approval

4.3 Credit Distribution
Credits must include:
- Core courses: 60% of total credits
- Electives: 30% of total credits
- Project/Internship: 10% of total credits


SECTION 5: ACADEMIC PROBATION
------------------------------

5.1 Probation Criteria
Students with CGPA below 5.0 for two consecutive semesters will be placed on academic probation.

5.2 Probation Consequences
Students on probation must:
- Meet with academic advisor bi-weekly
- Take reduced course load (maximum 18 credits)
- Achieve minimum CGPA of 5.5 in probation semester

5.3 Continuation
Students who fail to achieve required CGPA during probation may be asked to discontinue from the program.


END OF DOCUMENT
